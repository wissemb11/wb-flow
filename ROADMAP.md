## Not this quarter
We are currently under a 90-day feature freeze. Please refer to [../broadcast/20_FREEZE.md](../broadcast/20_FREEZE.md) for details on what is explicitly forbidden and what is permitted during this time. New features and commands will not be accepted.

## How we measure success
We track funnel metrics to ensure real usage and adoption. GitHub stars are considered an output metric of a successful ecosystem, not a goal to be gamed.

## Adoption milestones
- **Phase 1: 100 real users** (Installed and produced at least one plan file they kept)
- **Phase 2: 1k weekly workflows** (Plan files)
- **Phase 3: 10 contributors** (Merged PRs)
- **Phase 4: 1k stars** (Without giveaways or star-for-star schemes)
