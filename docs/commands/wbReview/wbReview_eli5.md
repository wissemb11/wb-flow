# /wbReview — ELI5

You asked someone to clean the kitchen. They say they're done.

`/wbReview` is you going into the kitchen with the list you gave them, checking each item: the dishes are done ✅, the counters wiped ✅, the floor mopped ❌ (still sticky in the corner).

The key difference from `/wbAudit`:
- **`/wbAudit`** — you walk into *any* kitchen and judge if it's clean.
- **`/wbReview`** — you walk into *this* kitchen and check if *the list you gave* was followed.

Review needs the plan. Audit doesn't. If you don't have a plan, you want audit, not review.

Use review when an AI (or you) finished executing a `/wbPlan` and you need to verify it actually happened vs. just ticked the checkboxes.
