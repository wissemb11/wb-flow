# /wbVision — ELI5

You can't think of what to build next. You point the AI at a package (or the whole monorepo) and ask: "what should I build?"

`/wbVision` gives you 5-10 ideas. Honest warning: most of them will be bad (obvious, generic, or too big). But 1 or 2 might be genuinely good. Your job is to pick those.

Don't treat the list as a roadmap. Treat it as a brainstorm assist. Keep 1, throw the rest away.

Best used rarely. Daily brainstorming kills your own sense of what matters.
